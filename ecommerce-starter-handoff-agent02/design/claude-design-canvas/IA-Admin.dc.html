Admin navigation
  • Desktop: left sidebar grouped as Ringkasan · Penjualan · Katalog · Marketing · Analitik · Pengaturan

  • Global search: order number, SKU, product name, customer (masked per role)
  • Alert centre: low stock, payment expiring, failed deliveries, courier updates overdue
