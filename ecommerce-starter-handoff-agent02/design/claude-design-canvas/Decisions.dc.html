Working assumptions

  1. No guest checkout: login is mandatory before the Address step.
  2. One store, one stock location and one shipping origin per client fork.
  3. Displayed prices are final prices including tax (confirmed by D5).
  4. No quantitative business KPI (key performance indicator) targets exist; only measurement definitions are proposed.
  5. All demo people, addresses and phone numbers are fictional; no live personal data in fixtures.
  6. Imagery uses neutral placeholders or original illustrations; no reference-brand assets.
  7. Prototype state may persist in browser local storage, disclosed as prototype-only, with a "reset demo data" control.
  8. Analytics event payloads carry no PII (personally identifiable information): no names, emails, phones, addresses or birth dates.
  9. "Delivered" comes only from a simulated courier event; admins may move paid orders to Processing and Shipped.
  10. Validation widths 360 / 390 / 768 / 1280 / 1440 px; implementation breakpoints proposed at M3.
  11. Storefront copy in Bahasa Indonesia; design documentation in English.

Measurement definitions (no targets)

Approved with D12. Targets remain undefined; formulas are written up in ANALYTICS_METRICS.md at M5.
Revenue = Σ grand total of orders with payment status Paid in range − Σ refunded amounts recorded in range.
Orders = count of orders whose payment became Paid in range (cancelled/unpaid excluded).
AOV (average order value) = Revenue ÷ Orders.
Conversion rate = sessions with a purchase event ÷ all sessions in range.
Abandoned cart rate = sessions with add_to_cart and no purchase ÷ sessions with add_to_cart.
Repeat purchase rate = customers with ≥2 paid orders (lifetime, at least one in range) ÷ customers with ≥1 paid order in range.
Top products = ranked by units sold on paid orders; revenue shown as secondary column.
Campaign attribution = last campaign touch (UTM/promotion click) within 7 days before purchase (mock rule).
