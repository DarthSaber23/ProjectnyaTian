Navigation model

Shared (all brands)
  • Announcement bar (dismissible, CMS-driven)
  • Header: logo, category entry, search, wishlist, account, cart with count
  • Cart drawer (S09) opens from any page after add-to-cart
  • Footer: customer service, bulk inquiry, shipping info, policies, blog, newsletter, social links
  • Breadcrumbs on catalog, product, blog and content pages
  • Floating "Hubungi CS" (WhatsApp link-out) on product, cart and order pages
Brand A — Cosmetics
  • Editorial top nav: Belanja per Kebutuhan Kulit (skin concern), Koleksi, Bundel, Jurnal (blog), Tentang
  • Concern and product-type entry are both category trees; collections are curated
  • Mobile: menu sheet with concern tiles first; search behind an icon
Brand B — Accessories
  • Mega-menu by product type (Kabel, Adaptor & Hub, Charger, Penyimpanan, Aksesori Meja)
  • Second entry "Cari per Konektor" (USB Type-C, Lightning, HDMI, DisplayPort) as filtered catalog views
  • Mobile: search bar always visible under the header; category list in a sheet
URL and SEO rules
Filter and sort state is kept in query parameters (?warna=hitam&urut=termurah). Filtered pages point their canonical URL to the unfiltered category page; paginated pages (?page=2) keep their own canonical. Search results (/cari) and account, cart, checkout and payment routes are excluded from indexing (noindex) and from the sitemap.xml specification. Brand A's concern pages and Brand B's connector pages are real category URLs, so they can rank.
